# UX audit: what the person sees (2026-09-01)

Walked production (vitals.chocksy.com) signed in as the owner, every
page top to bottom, plus the database behind both accounts. Screenshots
in `/tmp/audit/`. Every root cause below was read in the code or the
data, not guessed.

## The one-paragraph diagnosis

The engine is good and the pages leak its internals. Five surfaces ask
the same question in different words; risk scores wear the same clothes
as diagnoses; the genome's best sentences are buried under a file name;
a year of phone data walked in and the app dressed it up as 3,266 blood
draws. Nothing moves when you answer something, so the one thing that
should feel alive (your picture changing because you told it something)
is invisible. The fix is not more features: it is one place to be asked,
one ranking that respects certainty, one home for each kind of data, and
motion that shows cause and effect.

## Findings, ranked by how much they break the experience

### 1. "Atherosclerosis takes over both homepages" — the ranking ignores certainty

**Seen:** owner Home #1 insulin resistance 64 %, #2 atherosclerotic risk
43 %. Ramona's data: iron deficiency **92.6 %, confirmed**, yet
atherosclerotic risk 49 % and Lp(a) 50 % outrank it in the lifespan
lens. Type 2 diabetes sits at 9 % for the owner but is the biggest bubble
on `/graph` ("2-hour OGTT with…") and the second item on "Next draw".

**Why (verified):** `lib/ledger.ts` sorts conclusions by
`matters = score × lensWeight` (lines 195–197, 828–834), default lens
`lifespan`. `ascvd_risk` carries lifespan weight 3, `iron_deficiency`
does not, so 0.49 × 3 beats 0.93 × 1. A confirmed finding loses to a
"possible" risk score by construction. Separately, `ascvd_risk`,
`cancer_screening_due` and `low_fitness_sarcopenia` are _states_, not
diseases, but the cards render them identically ("Atherosclerotic risk:
possible").

**Fix:** rank by state band first (confirmed › likely › possible ›
unlikely), lens weight only _within_ a band. Give risk-states their own
visual grammar: a "RISK" chip and the word "risk" in the title, never
"possible atherosclerosis". The OGTT/T2D prominence on the graph is the
information-gain engine correctly saying "this test would settle
things", but the page never says that; label it "worth testing", not
size-as-belief.

### 2. Questions are everywhere and nowhere

**Seen:** the waist question appears on Home card 1, Home card 4, three
times in the `/graph` side panel (once per condition it moves), on
`/plan` and on `/review`. The Home counter says "21 open questions, each
one changes a conclusion" and links to `#first-question` (one question);
`/feel` says "3 of 22 answered"; `/review` has 1 profile question and 10
"values the lab sheet did not settle" (Keep / Discard a stored reading
whose sheet line reads "not found"). The high-blood-pressure card shows
its question twice (an input and a "(free)" test chip).

**Why:** three independent askers (`profileQuestions`, `symptomAsks`,
the curator's `review_items`) each render wherever they are called;
nothing dedupes across cards or pages; the counter counts a union the
person cannot see. The curator questions are the exact thing the owner
was promised the second-model verification would settle (phase 7
raw-sheet verify exists; "not found" rows should go to a smarter
re-read, not to the person).

**Fix:** ONE asking surface. The Today card asks the single best
question (already engine-chosen); every other place shows "1 question
would move this → answer it" as a link to that card, never a second
input. Counter = questions the engine would ask today (small, honest).
Curator "not found" rows: second-pass OCR + a stronger model with the
raw crop; only genuine ties reach the person, and they arrive in the
same Today card with a picture of the sheet line, not a table of
Keep/Discard. Per-question dedupe in the graph panel with the combined
deltas ("waist → IR 64→81, HBP 35→49, MASLD 40→20").

### 3. Phone data is treated as blood

**Seen:** Labs › Draws: "3266 blood draws, newest first", one card per
day of resting HR / sleep, burying the 17 real PDFs. History: the facts
lane is a wall of struck-through "resting hr 48 / 55 / 52…". Today at
06:18 shows "STEPS 49 · ACTIVE 1.2 kcal · STAND 1 h" as if the day were
done; Quick Numbers has sleep blank while the sleep card says 6.27 h;
the consistency heatmap is solid blue for a year because the phone
"logs" every day.

**Why (verified):** `getDraws` (`lib/daily-data.ts:387`) groups every
reading by `observedAt` with no `source` filter. `FACT_FROM_READING`
(`lib/healthkit.ts:910`) rewrites `resting_hr` as a dated fact on every
sync where the value differs, which for a heart rate is every day, so
`writeFact` closes and opens a history row daily. Today reads the day's
partial aggregates without saying "so far". The heatmap counts any
daily_logs row as activity.

**Fix:** Draws = lab-sourced readings only; wearables get their own
place: a "Phone" tab under Labs with per-metric daily charts (they are
time series, not draws), and `/m/<code>` for wearable codes shows a
daily line, not dots. System facts from continuous signals must not
write history: keep the reading, derive the fact at read time (7-day
median) or write it monthly. Today: "so far · synced 06:18" on partial
days and yesterday's complete strip above it; fill Quick Numbers from
the same daily row the cards read. Heatmap counts human input only (or
gets a "phone" mode). Sleep is fine in the data (776 nights since 2022);
the app just never showed it.

### 4. The genome's best sentences are buried

**Seen:** `/uploads/<id>` for the genome file is the best-written page in
the app: "Coeliac disease is essentially excluded", "TCF7L2 CT: about
40 % above background risk, and the DPP trial showed lifestyle change
erases most of it", "FTO AA: roughly 2.4 kg more body weight through
appetite; activity blunts most of it", CYP1A2 fast metaboliser, lactase
non-persistent. None of it appears on Home, Plan or the condition cards
except as machine tokens ("prior ×1.4").

**Fix:** after a genome upload, the ledger gets a "What your genome
changed" card for a week (the same sentences, three of them, with "see
all 11"), each condition card's FOR/AGAINST lists the human sentence
("TCF7L2 CT raises the prior") instead of `hypothesis:x 0.637`, and the
graph's gene bubbles link to the same text. Same treatment for any
document upload: its findings surface where they act, not only where
they were filed.

### 5. Home composition leaks the engine

**Seen:** card 1 has a ~150 px blank region where a trend chart should
be ("glucose, 45 draws" caption under nothing) — a render bug. Cards
6–10 are bare "Cholesterol, Total 217 mg/dL, off" with two collapsed
stubs `WHY` / `NOT RIGHT?` and nothing else — filler. "Since Aug 31,
2026: 0 resolved · 0 new · 0 stronger · 0 weaker" is a line of zeros.
Systems show "0" in a green ring for Liver, Kidneys, Sex hormones — a
zero reads as empty, not healthy. FOR/AGAINST lines are tokens
(`tgHdl 2.12`, `family_history no`, `hypothesis:insulin_resistance
0.637`). Badges `ENERGY B · WEIGHT A · LIFESPAN A` and "TIP OF THE SPEAR"
are internal vocabulary.

**Fix:** fix the chart; collapse "off" markers into one row per system
("3 lipid markers off → Lipids") instead of five cards; drop the zeros
line when everything is zero; systems ring shows a check, not "0";
FOR/AGAINST in sentences ("triglyceride/HDL ratio 2.1 (for)", "no family
history (against)"); lens badges become one line "matters most for
lifespan"; rename the spear "Start here".

### 6. Nothing moves, so nothing feels caused

**Seen:** answering a question reloads the page; percentages change with
no continuity; the ledger reorders silently; "since yesterday" is text.
The person cannot see that their answer did something, which is the
whole promise of the interview.

**Fix (from the two motion skills, mapped to real moments):**

| Moment                                       | Transition                                            | Notes                                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| A belief % changes after an answer           | number pop-in (02)                                    | `tabular-nums` on every % so nothing shifts       |
| A state chip flips (possible → likely)       | text states swap (04)                                 | colour transitions with it                        |
| Cards reorder in the ledger                  | card resize (01) + FLIP reorder                       | interruptible CSS, `transition-property` explicit |
| Fact saved / chip confirmed                  | success check (10) inside the button, then toast (22) | scale on press 0.96 on every button               |
| Today card question answered → next question | panel reveal (07)                                     | the answered one collapses softly                 |
| Composer chips appearing while typing        | texts reveal (18), 40 ms stagger                      | dashed → solid on post via icon swap (09)         |
| Lens / tab switching                         | tabs sliding (16)                                     | one pill, not two toggles                         |
| Page load with data                          | skeleton reveal (14), `initial={false}` afterwards    | no enter animation on plain navigation            |

Also from the interface checklist: the `WHY` / `NOT RIGHT?` and
"This changed / I was wrong" links are ~12 px tall (min hit area is
40×40); nested cards share the same radius (concentric rule); dynamic
numbers need `tabular-nums`; headings need `text-wrap: balance`.

### 7. Sync: real data, wrong story on screen

**Data says:** the full resync landed — 12,119 readings over 3,260
distinct days, sleep 776 nights back to 2022, 10 metrics. Server logs
show no failed sync request in 36 h. "Only 7 things" is what the Sync
tab shows per type, not what arrived; the "failed" toast was
client-side (a very large day batch or a timeout mid-run; the anchors
resume, which is why the data is complete anyway).

**Fix:** Sync tab shows totals that match the server ("12,119 readings ·
3,260 days"), a progress line during resync ("2019 … 2026, day 1,204 of
3,260"), per-batch retry with backoff, and an honest "resumed" instead
of "failed" when a batch retries.

### 8. Small but real

- `/graph` right after cutover said "needs sex and age" for the owner
  although both facts existed; it works now. Watch for a cache/timing
  path on first load.
- Prod log: an hourly failed sign-in for `lab-orch@localhost.local` —
  something is probing auth; find and stop it.
- "Failed to find Server Action" after each deploy — stale tabs; harmless
  but a reload nudge would be kinder.
- History replay shows the person's beliefs as a plain text line; the
  lane is right, the number strip could be the same animated % chips.

## What I could not see

Ramona's pages (no credentials; her data was read directly), the iOS
Sync tab on the device, and phone-width screenshots of the web (the app
webview covers that path).

## Proposed order (phase 24, one change at a time, each with a lock)

1. **24a Ranking and one asking surface** — state-band ranking, risk
   grammar, Today card as the single asker, counters unified, graph
   panel dedupe. Lock: a ledger test (confirmed beats possible under any
   lens), a journey asserting the ranking, a page test that the same
   question key renders at most once per page.
2. **24b Phone data has its own home** — Draws = labs; Phone tab and
   daily metric charts; system facts stop writing history; Today
   partial-day honesty; heatmap semantics. Lock: `getDraws` filters by
   source (test); history test that a daily sync writes zero fact rows.
3. **24c Findings surface where they act** — genome/document "what
   changed" card, human FOR/AGAINST, gene sentences on the graph. Lock:
   snapshot test of a card's FOR/AGAINST strings containing no
   `hypothesis:` / `_` tokens.
4. **24d Home composition + motion** — chart bug, filler collapse,
   zeros, systems ring, the transition table above, hit areas, tabular
   numbers. Lock: visual checks + the transitions' reduced-motion guard
   present.
5. **24e Curator second pass** — "not found" rows re-read by a stronger
   model with the crop; only ties to the person, in the Today card.
   Lock: eval on the 10 current rows (how many settle without a human).
6. **24f Sync truthfulness** — totals, progress, retry, "resumed".
