Illustrative, with real shapes and units: the Apple Health day — 8 412 steps, 34 min exercise, 9 of 12 stand hours, RHR 55 bpm, HRV 48 ms, sleep 7 h 12, VO₂max 41.2 mL/kg/min, SpO₂ 97 %, weight 78.4 kg, body fat 17.8 % — and the 90 daily resting-heart-rate values behind the chart. The consistency grid is real from Jun 14 2026 and empty before it.

Build cost

Replaces: /today (day-stepper header, streak pill, WearableStrip ×2, NutritionLine, HabitChecklist, QuickNumbers, DailySparks, ConsistencyHeatmap), /feel (progress line, one card per group, FactEditButtons), /trends (DailyCharts on recharts, static pill tabs) and /history (HistoryLanes, the raw event table).

New: the source column — every phone row now prints the HealthKit type and the day it landed, so a blank waist reads as a denied permission and not as a zero. The twelve questions become one screen instead of a wizard, and each says whether answering would move anything.

Deletes: recharts (the daily charts are hand-drawn SVG), the static .pill-tabs family, the accent-ramp heatmap fills, the raw event table (the lanes carry it), and four routes.
