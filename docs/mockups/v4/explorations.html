Where the numbers come from

Real: every marker, band and date on the receipt and the one-pager; the Aug 1 2026 draw with 15 results (1 optimal, 9 normal, 5 off); the TPO, LDL and ALT pairs against the Dec 9 2025 draw; Hashimoto's moving 62 % → 95 % on the Aug 1 antibody result with a likelihood ratio of 4.2; the five likelihoods; the three running protocol items and their start dates; the Nov 24 2026 order sheet; the 27 Apple Health types.

Illustrative, with real shapes and units: the seven resting-HR bar heights on the wrist frame (Aug 26 → Sep 1), which come from the same illustrative daily series as the home signal panel, ending on the real 55 bpm against the real 90-day mean of 58.
