Chart hover

Phase 31b. One card, two triggers. Every mark on every chart and every value on every ruler answers the same five questions when you point at it: what day, what number, in what unit, what state that is, and what it was before. Nothing on a chart is a number without a date any more.

05

Where the numbers come from

All real: TPO antibodies 412 IU/mL on Dec 9 2025 and 320 on Aug 1 2026, normal 0–34, optimal 0–9, target under 100 by Feb 16 2027, with a draw planned for Nov 24 2026 and a projected 200–300 IU/mL if the current rate holds; LDL cholesterol 168 mg/dL on Dec 9 2025 and 131 on Aug 1 2026, goal band 70–100 by Dec 1 2026. The −22 % on both cards is the arithmetic between those two pairs. Every date on this page is a real draw date or a real target date.

Nothing here is illustrative. The page adds no data; it adds a way to read data that is already on the charts.

Build cost

Exists today. All of it, on the data side. The shared Ruler and HistoryChart shipped in phase 30c and each mark already knows its date, its value, its unit, the bands it is judged against and the previous value — that is what the “was” mark and the dated axis are drawn from. The state word comes from the same status function the rows use.

Does not exist. The interaction. Both components render static SVG with no pointer handling, no focusable marks and no positioned overlay. There is no shared card component.

What the app must gain. One <ChartHover> component holding {date, value, unit, state, band, was}, and, on each mark, a hit area, tabIndex, an aria-label carrying the same five facts as text, and a pointer and focus handler. Flip logic: above the plot's midline the card goes below the mark and the stem flips; within 21 px of an edge the card clamps and the stem slides. On the phone the card spans the plot and closes on the next tap outside it. No endpoint, no table, no migration.
