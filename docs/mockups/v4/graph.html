Where the numbers come from

All real, as of Sep 1 2026: the seven likelihoods — insulin resistance 97 %, Hashimoto's 95 %, chronic inflammation 77 %, iron deficiency 76 %, type 2 diabetes 53 %, fitness low 28 %, cardiovascular risk 21 % — and the markers behind each: TPO 320 IU/mL (ceiling 34), TSH 3.9 mIU/L (optimal 0.5–2.5), HbA1c 5.6 % (normal 4.0–5.6), glucose 96 mg/dL from the Aug 31 strip, hs-CRP 3.4 mg/L (optimal under 1), ferritin 22 ng/mL (optimal 50–150), LDL 142 mg/dL (optimal under 100), systolic BP 128 mmHg (normal 90–120), vitamin D 19 ng/mL (optimal 40–80), ALT 34 U/L (normal 0–33), creatinine 0.95 mg/dL (0.6–1.2), cortisol 16.29 mcg/dL (6–18.4), red cell count 5.6 M/uL (4.5–5.9), VO₂max 41.2 mL/kg/min. Sex hormones has never been measured. Edges: Hollowell 2002, NHANES III, OR 8.4 (95 % CI 6.9 to 10.2); the HbA1c edge is an engine rule and says so. The family-history ×3 was recorded Sep 1 2026.

Build cost

Replaces: /graph — ViewShell, the lens PillTabs on URL params, the bubbles/systems toggle, the pan-and-zoom SVG stage, the side panel with AskBox and AskLink questions, Patterns matched, Hot nodes, Active edges, the blocked state and the empty state.

New: the systems lens is twelve arcs on one rule — where the driving marker sits inside its own range — instead of twelve tiles with a status badge; every edge row prints its effect size and confidence interval, or says it is a rule and not a paper.

Deletes: StatusBadge, the filled health badges, the second tab style, and the blocked-state screen (the missing facts are rows in Coverage on Plan, where they can be answered).
