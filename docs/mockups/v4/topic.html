Topic

Phase 35. A topic is a named thing you take, do or wonder about: creatine, omega-3, cold exposure, psyllium. The page reads what the trials found per outcome, grades each one by its design, and keeps the contrary evidence beside the supporting evidence — including the claim that is only an association, printed as plainly as the trials and labelled so it can never be read as a cause.

01

The topic, and the verdict strip

The title is the topic as you typed it. Under it, one sentence saying why this topic is on your file at all, and the run line. Then one row per outcome, grouped by direction, best grade first. Direction is good or bad by the outcome and not by up or down: an adverse outcome going up is off, however strong the trial behind it.

02

What the trials found, and what is only an association

Two columns on the desktop, stacked on the phone. A finding row is the paper row from section 15 with four more facts in the citation line — dose, duration, the population with its n, and the study type in words — the effect as the sentence, and the paper's own sentence under it. Nothing is paraphrased into a stronger claim than the abstract made.

What the trials found

4 of 14 papers · 12 outcomes
Creatine monohydrate and maximal strength in resistance-trained adults
Bergström · J Appl Physiol · 2019 ● A randomised, n = 46 5 g/day · 12 weeks trained adults, 24–48 y

One-rep-max bench press rose 7.4 % against placebo, 95 % CI +3.1 to +11.7, and grip rose 3.2 kg against 0.6 kg on placebo.

quote → “Handgrip strength increased by 3.2 kg in the creatine arm and by 0.6 kg on placebo over twelve weeks of identical training.”
Lower-body strength after creatine loading: a meta-analysis of twenty-two randomised trials
Vasquez · Sports Med · 2021 ● A meta-analysis, 22 trials, n = 721 3–5 g/day · 4–24 weeks healthy adults

A pooled standardised mean difference of 0.29, 0.17 to 0.41, for leg press and squat. Small, consistent, and it does not grow past twelve weeks.

quote → “The pooled effect was small but heterogeneity was low, and no trial longer than twelve weeks reported a further gain.”
Creatine and working memory after twenty-four hours without sleep
Nakamura · Neuropsychopharmacology · 2022 ● B randomised crossover, n = 24 20 g/day · 7 days rested and sleep-deprived, same people

Backward digit span held at baseline after a night awake, where placebo lost 1.4 digits. Rested performance did not change at all.

quote → “The benefit appeared only in the sleep-deprived condition; rested performance was indistinguishable between arms.”
Total body water and lean mass through the first week of creatine loading
Osei · Med Sci Sports Exerc · 2018 ● A randomised, n = 32 20 g/day · 7 days untrained men and women

Body mass rose 1.1 kg in the first week and deuterium dilution put all of it in total body water. Fat mass did not move.

quote → “The entire mass gain over seven days was accounted for by intracellular water; no change in fat mass was detected.”

What is only an association

1 paper · nothing here moves a number
Reported dietary creatine intake and prostate cancer in a national nutrition survey
Ferreira · Nutr Cancer · 2017 ◐ C cross-sectional survey, n = 892 no dose recorded men, 40–75 y

The highest intake quartile carried an odds ratio of 1.4, 1.05 to 1.9, against the lowest. The intake was a food questionnaire, not a supplement log.

association a survey of what people ate, not a trial; it cannot say creatine causes it

The column is never hidden and never collapsed, and it keeps its heading when it is empty. A page that shows only the helpful trials is an advertisement; this column is what stops this one becoming that.

07

Where the numbers come from

Real, from the test account: creatine 5 g a day as a protocol item since Jun 14 2026; grip strength 38 kg on the Aug 1 2026 draw against a 45 kg aim; the Nov 24 2026 retest date; inflammation at borderline, which is what puts omega-3 on the list; the four topic origins (adopted, goal, asked, typed) and the 30-day topic cadence, both from the phase 35 spec.

Placeholder, and clearly so: every citation on this page — Bergström 2019, Vasquez 2021, Nakamura 2022, Osei 2018, Ferreira 2017, Ito 2020, Lindqvist 2023 — with its journal, its n, its effect size and its quoted sentence. They are written to be plausible in shape, not to be looked up. A real implementation fills every one of them from the topic run, and the page is drawn so that swapping the text changes nothing about the layout. The counts (14 papers read, 12 outcomes, 6 psyllium papers found) are the same kind of placeholder.

Build cost

What this page needs. The topic_watch and topic_findings tables from migration 0027, lib/topic-watch.ts beside the existing research-watch.ts, the extra outcomeText field on the extraction item schema so a non-marker outcome like “working memory” survives, and relevanceOf(topic, person), which is one sentence built from the person's own rows and never a model call.

What it reuses. The paper row, the state words, the tables and the empty tiles are all already in system.css. The feed rows go to the existing paper_watch with conditionId = "topic:<topic>", so the Research list and the phone show them with no new plumbing. Grades come from the existing gradeOf; a cross-sectional study lands at C and the page says association instead of inventing a softer word for it.

What it must never do. Nothing on this page writes hkb_interventions and nothing here moves a probability. A topic finding is knowledge about a substance, not a belief about a person, and the two stay in different tables for exactly that reason.
