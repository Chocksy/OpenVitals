Systems — state, not links

Twelve instruments on a panel. Each one reads out its own state word in its own colour, the marker driving it, that marker's value and unit, and a 2 px tick for the direction. No chevrons, no underlines, no arrows: the whole tile is tappable and none of it looks like a link.
