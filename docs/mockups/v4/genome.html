TCF7L2

rs7903146 C/T ● A 36 % of Europeans carry at least one T

You carry one copy of the T allele. This is the strongest common variant for type 2 diabetes anyone has found: it changes how much insulin your pancreas releases after a meal, not how your muscles answer it. One copy raises the starting odds by about 40 %.

What it changes for you: your T2D likelihood started at 38 % rather than 27 % before any blood was read. Your HbA1c of 5.6 and your missing fasting insulin are doing the rest of the work.

trending_up Type 2 diabetes ×1.4 per T allele · ● A Grant 2006 · Nat Genet · replicated

A variant shifts a starting point; your numbers decide the rest. If a marker and a variant disagree, the marker wins, because it is what your body is doing today.
