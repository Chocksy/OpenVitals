Where the numbers come from

Real: the four adopted protocol items and their adherence — selenium 200 µg at 86 %, cut added sugar at 80 % over 30 days, vitamin D 4 000 IU at 100 %, sardines three tins a week at 0 % since Jun 14 2026; the goals and their due dates — TPO under 100 IU/mL by Feb 16 2027 with 320 today from 412 on Dec 9 2025 and 29 % of the way, ferritin above 50 ng/mL by Nov 24 2026 with 22 today, vitamin D 40–60 ng/mL by Nov 24 2026 with 19 today and no retest yet; LDL 131 mg/dL on Aug 1 2026 from 168 on Dec 9 2025 against a 70–100 goal band due Dec 1 2026; HbA1c 5.6 % on Aug 1 2026, unchanged from Dec 9 2025; the planned Nov 24 2026 draw with four markers; the retest cadences hs-CRP 4 weeks, TSH 6 weeks, LDL 8 weeks, HbA1c and TPO 12 weeks, and TSH 3.9 mIU/L last read Aug 1 2026; resting heart rate 55 bpm on three-session weeks against 58 on the others; the 30-cell strip for added sugar, 24 of 30.

Illustrative: every clock time — 07:10, 08:00, 12:30, 13:10, 17:30, 19:30, 21:00 — because no protocol item carries a time of day. The training days (Mon, Thu, Sat), the sardine days (Tue, Fri, Sun) and the alternate-day parity of the iron are placements, not stored schedules. The 10 000-step row's 17 of 30 and the 8 400 median are the shape of a real daily series, not a read one. The two suggestions — ten minutes outside, twelve minutes of walking — are labelled “suggested” on the page precisely because nothing in the engine proposed them.

Build cost

Exists today. protocol_items holds the adopted item, its why, its metricCodes, a cadence text column, active and startedAt. habit_logs holds one tick per item per day, unique on (itemId, day), written by POST /api/habits, and adherence() in lib/daily.ts already turns those into the 86 %, the 80 % and the 30-cell strip this page reuses. goals holds the target band and the due date. The plan's actions carry dose {amount, form, schedule, duration, ceiling}, a timing string, targets[].measureAfterWeeks and followUp[].afterDays, and followUp already mints checkins rows.

Does not exist. Seven things, and every one of them is a column that is not there. One: no time of day. protocol_items has no time, hour or slot, and ReportAction.timing is free text that nothing parses. Two: no weekday selector. cadence is a single text column and adopt() only ever writes "weekly" or "daily" into it, by testing the dose string against /week/i. There is no days-of-week array, no RRULE, no bitmask, so “Mon, Thu, Sat” and “alternate days” cannot be stored. Three: no structured dose. Dose is one flattened string, "200 µg · capsule · once daily · for 6 months". There is no amount, unit or times-per-day. Four: no “with what”. “With food”, “empty stomach” and “4 h clear of the iron” appear in the repo only inside prose strings; there is no column and no enum. Five: no “until”. protocol_items has startedAt and no endsAt; dose.duration is text and is never turned into a date. That is why the sardines row has run eleven weeks at 0 %. Six: no dose-level tick. habit_logs is one boolean per item per day, so it structurally cannot record “two of three doses”. Seven: no occurrence rows. Nothing materialises a (item, day) row ahead of time, so the month strip's dots have nothing to read; a habit_logs row only appears after a tap.

What the engine must gain. Columns on protocol_items: slot (morning / midday / evening / bedtime), days_of_week, interval (for alternate days), dose_amount, dose_unit, times_per_day, with_what and ends_at. One generator that expands those into occurrences for a date range — it can stay a pure function over the columns, no table needed, as long as habit_logs gains a slot so a three-dose day can be half done. And one place to put the plan's lines: today they live inside reports.body.actions jsonb, so any per-line state has to go into that blob or into a new table.
