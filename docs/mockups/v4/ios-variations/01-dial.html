Dial

Variation 01 of the phase-36 phone, from the seed jmH9kKIdVaUqldfYCAlV3xB3. An instrument you can read from across the room. Every number that has a target gets a scale and a needle; the needle means the same thing on a goal row, a macro bar and the projection chart, so one mark is one idea everywhere.

Everything here runs. Press Play and the Add flow plays inside the phone — typed, spoken, one photograph, several photographs. Tick a move chip and the dial fills and the flame reacts. Toggle a habit or a food rule under the expectation chart and the projection redraws and the landing number counts to its new value. Drag across the chart and the tooltip follows your finger.

00

Direction

Read the seed as a designer would. Three digits: 9 … 3 x 3. It says nine twice, once as a number and once as a product, so the page is built on threes and the phone is a three-column grid where every block is one, two or three cells wide. A word hides in the middle of it: C-A-l in fYCAlV3xB3, and right after it V3 and B3 — calories, and two vitamins. Another word falls out of the letters in order: d, i, a, l. The case never rests for more than two characters, so the type never changes size to make a point; it changes weight.

The seed

jmH9kKIdVaUqldfYCAlV3xB3

Printed with the parts that carry the direction picked out: the nine, the letters d · i · a · l, the hidden CAL, and the 3 × 3 that says nine again.

Mood
A measuring instrument, not a dashboard. It says where the needle is now and where it lands if you hold.
Accent
Instrument blue #2f5170, one accent, never a word. It tints the dial track, the hairline index ticks, the projection line and the chart wash. Lime stays on the add control and the scan line; the spectrum stays a word or a mark.
Type
The five sizes in a 3 : 2 : 1 rhythm — three 11 px mono labels and two 13 px lines for every 15 px sentence, and 34 px only in the middle of a dial. Weight carries emphasis, never size.
Density
Three columns of 8 px gutters on a 364 px phone. 13 px inner padding on the phone, 21 px on the web. Nine blocks fit above the fold on Today.
Cards
No shadow. A hairline box with an index tick in the top-left corner, the mark an instrument face carries. Radii 21 outside, 13 inside. The only fills are the ticked chip (ink) and the dial wash.
Motion
One easing, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). One duration family in thirds: 90 · 270 · 810 ms. Every animation on the page is one of those three, or a 2 200 ms loop for the two states genuinely waiting on a network call.
The unusual idea
The landing needle. One mark, used four times. On a goal row it is where the target sits on the scale. On a macro bar it is the day's target. On the dial it is the calorie goal. On the expectation chart it is the value the projection lands on, and it physically travels — with the number counting under it — the moment you adopt or drop a habit. Nothing else on the page is a vertical 2 px ink line, so the mark can never be read as anything else.

02

Add — played, four ways in

The complaint was the four seconds between pressing Send and seeing anything. So this is not four frames, it is the four seconds. Pick a way in and press Play: the sentence types itself, or the mic opens and the waveform runs while the words arrive, or one photograph is scanned and labelled, or three photographs are read one after the other. The reading loader and the receipt are the same in all four, so the app has one waiting state and not four.

Add

Three photographs are one meal, not three. The strip reads them left to right, each thumbnail lights lime while it is the one being looked at, and the three totals are added once at the end.

Lime appears twice outside a control: the scan line and the thumbnail that is being read. Both are the app putting data in, which is what lime has always meant here.

03

The expectation — what holding this does

The quick view after a meal or a tick. The day first: calories against the target, protein, and the shape of the day in three meals and five moves. Then the sentence and the chart. Toggle a habit or a food rule and the projection redraws, the landing needle travels and the number counts to it. Drag across the plot and the tooltip follows your finger. The arithmetic is the engine's: grade-weighted effect × adherence × horizon, clamped at the marker's MAX_CHANGE.

07

Motion, data, credits

One easing everywhere, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). One duration family in thirds: 90, 270 and 810 ms. The only exceptions are the two loops that are genuinely waiting on a network call, and they run at 2 200 ms so they read as waiting rather than as decoration.

What moves Duration Trigger
The dial fills · .dial .val 810 ms a chip is ticked, or the screen appears
The chip presses · .movechip 270 ms · .96 → 1.03 → 1 tap down
The check draws in 270 ms, 90 ms delay the tick write is accepted, never before it
The dial flashes lime 270 ms in, 810 ms out the same accepted write, once
The flame flares · .flame.lit 810 ms the fifth move lands and the day closes
The flame breathes 2 400 ms, loops the streak is one day or more; still at zero
The scan line · .scanline 2 200 ms, loops the photo is uploaded; stops when vision returns
The shimmer 2 200 ms, loops, linear same as the scan line, under it
The three dots · .loader3 810 ms, 90 ms apart Send is pressed, on every path
The waveform · .wave 810 ms, 45 ms apart the mic is open; still the moment it closes
A kept word arrives 270 ms, 90 ms apart each chip the reader returns, in its order
A callout arrives · .callout 270 ms, 520 ms apart the vision call returns items; one per item
The meal card rises 270 ms · 8 px the server total is accepted
The projection redraws · .expo .proj 810 ms a habit or food rule is toggled, or the horizon changes
The landing number counts 810 ms the same toggle; the needle travels with it
The tooltip follows · .hovercard 90 ms in, none while moving pointer down on the plot; it tracks with no easing
The swipe row opens 270 ms a tap here; a drag past 34 px on the phone

Reduced motion. One variant covers all of it. Every loop stops and shows its end state, the scan line is removed rather than frozen (a bright bar parked over a photograph reads as a defect), the check appears already drawn, chips and callouts appear at full opacity with no offset, the dial is drawn filled, and the projection, the landing number and the swipe row jump to their new values instead of travelling. Nothing is removed and no information is lost, because none of these animations carry anything the text does not.
