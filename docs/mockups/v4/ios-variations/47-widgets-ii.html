Widgets II

iOS variation 47 · round thirteen

Widgets on a drifting wallpaper, each in frosted glass and each answering one question named in its title. A 13-week calendar shows the past. Tap a widget and it zooms open; a plate opens a meal you can resize, fix or delete. Slide Do next to tick: 72 springs to 74.

MoodAn iPhone home screen on a soft day: drifting pastel wallpaper, glass widgets, only the island black.
AccentRing red, green, blue for the layers; Matrix hue for past days; orange for food; lime on +.
TypeSF Pro Rounded, Nunito as fallback. Heavy tabular numbers; 13 px titles phrased as the question.
Unusual ideaWidgets are doors: each zooms into its detail view (the iOS 18 zoom). A move is ticked by sliding it.

The direction's rules

Rule How
One question per widget How am I doing (rings), What I ate (kcal ring), Do next (the move), Where blood is going (the worst marker), Last 13 weeks (the calendar), Plates (each meal, tap to fix).
Worst marker, not LDL Each BLOOD row is parsed for its value and aim. Rank = distance past the aim edge as a share of that edge: LDL 31 %, vitamin D 28 %, ApoB 9 %. The top one shows, with its projection if one exists; the stack dots page to the next (vitamin D has no projection until the Nov 1 draw, and says so).
Glass on a wallpaper Five blurred blobs drift on 21 to 38 s loops. Widgets are 60 % white with a 21 px backdrop blur; secondary text is #4f4f56 so it stays above 4.5 : 1 on the lightest glass.
Zoom The detail view is clipped to the widget's rect and the clip opens to the full screen (clip-path inset, 560 ms, a slight overshoot). Home scales to 0.92 and blurs behind it. Close reverses it.
Slide to tick Drag the knob past 70 % of the pill: the check draws, the red ring springs, 72 counts to 74 and today's square in the calendar recolours. Short of 70 % it springs back.
Meal edit Portion ¼ to 2 in steps; kcal and macros follow and every widget updates. Fix results offers the three usual corrections and re-reads; Delete asks once and takes the kcal off today.
Past days Streak's generator (mulberry32, seed 435) and its colour rule hsl(30 + s/100 · 110, 68%, 90 − (s − 55) · 2.4 %). Today pulses; the Aug 1 draw has a white dot.
