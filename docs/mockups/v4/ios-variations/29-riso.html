Today printed as a two-drum zine: fluorescent pink and ultramarine on newsprint. There is no third value in the file. Every purple on the page is blue landing on pink, and the score is printed that way on purpose: one pink field, the numerals blue inside it, so the overprint is the largest thing on the sheet.

Two inks, no third value The file holds --paper, --pink and --blue. No black, no grey, no tinted greys: small type is full blue ink, the fields are full pink ink, and the phone frame is a 1.5 px printed rule. What looks like a third colour is an overlap, never a choice.
The overprint is the page The pink field runs up through the masthead and the numerals are set in blue on top of it, so #231058 covers the masthead and the whole score block rather than a hairline of it. Fig. 1's landing and page two's coupon print the same way. The one thing you see first is made of both drums.
One slip, whole drum translate(2.5px, -2px) on everything the pink drum prints: the two fields, the underprint on every rule and every cut line, the photo tint, the projected bar in Fig. 1. The rules fringe pink on their top edge the whole way down the page. A slipped drum, not a shadow on three headlines.
One screen, used large 8 px pitch, 15°, one dot size, in the CSS and in the SVG pattern. It appears three times: the projected bar, the plate photograph on page two, and the small photographs in the listings. A screen means "not solid ink": the projection is screened, the two draws behind it are not.
Ink and paper, not fills Every field prints through a 0.6 px blur, with roller streaks letting the stock show through and a cut edge instead of a machined one. The stock carries laid lines at 4 % of the blue. Hierarchy is scale and coverage: three display sizes (104, 40, 22), one text face at three sizes, one 6 px spacing scale.
