Observatory

Variation 16, round five, built to an ambitious brief: the phone is an astronomical instrument. The face is a deep navy that is not black, every rule on it is brass, every reading is cut in Geist Mono and the few words are set in Instrument Serif. The day is a planisphere — the night as the outer arc, the meals as dishes orbiting at the hour they happened, the moves as engraved ticks that light copper when they close. Adding a meal is taking a reading: a brass reticle, a sweep, engraved labels on what it found. The projection is a comet drawn from today's number to where it lands. Same data as every other variation, same Play, same arithmetic.

The read
Twenty-four characters, so a day again. One digit only, a 4, so the face carries four things and the Add flow four ways in. Three letters run in order at 16–18, r s t: a scale is a sequence, and every scale on this page is engraved in order rather than coloured in. The string ends W E S — three of the four cardinal points, and the fourth is missing. So the dial has no north written on it: midnight is the top and the only thing that says where you are is the brass hand at 20:10. OV sits at 7–8, in the middle, which is where it belongs.
Mood
Read the day the way you read a sky chart.
Colour
Night, #0a0f16, a navy-black with blue still in it, never #000. One metal: brass #c99a55, 6.9:1 on the face, and it is the only bright thing — the hand, a closed move, the comet's head, the reticle. One warning colour, #d4726b, on every word that says a marker is out; a state that is fine is not a colour but a neutral word, and nothing on the page is green. Lime is the one system rule this page keeps whole: it marks the control that writes data, so it sits on the add control in the tab bar and on the Send that exists only once there is something to send. The light theme is parchment and ink with the brass turned to sepia.
Type
Geist Mono carries every number, every unit and every engraved label, at 8–10 px with 0.12em tracking where the instrument names itself. Instrument Serif carries the five words a page needs: the masthead and the three screen names.
Imagery
Seven dishes, generated photoreal from above on the face's own background and cut to circles, so a meal is the meal and not a coloured arc. On the dial each one sits at its hour, held in a circle, and the largest is a tenth wider than the smallest: enough to weigh a plate, not enough to read as three sizes.
Density
No card anywhere. A surface is a hairline and a rectangle; nothing is rounded except a dish, a dot and the two round controls, because a lens is the only round thing on an instrument. Every gap is a rung of 3 5 8 13 21 34 55 89 144.
Motion
One easing, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1), and 120 / 240 / 480 / 960. The reticle sweeps once per read at 1 920 ms, which is two of the longest. Nothing glows and nothing shimmers.
Broken on purpose
The five-size type ladder. Instrument Serif runs at 34 px on the screen names and 55 px on the masthead — a sixth and a seventh size the system does not have — because an instrument face is named in engraving, not in UI type. Everything else on the page stays inside the five.

Where the numbers come from

Data. The same file as ios-2.html and the other variations: 52 markers with a value, 7 off, TPO 320 IU/mL against normal 0–34, vitamin D 19 ng/mL against 40–60, ferritin 22 ng/mL, LDL 118 mg/dL against the 70–100 goal band, the Aug 1 2026 draw, a 6-day streak with 18 as the best run, three meals on Saturday Sep 5 totalling 1 497 kcal est. and 93 / 93 / 84 g, steps 10 935 against a 90-day mean of 11 062, exercise 43 min against 49, three strength sessions on file for the week. The projection is lib/projection.ts run in the browser on the seeded interventions: effect × grade weight (A and B 1, C 0.5, D and E 0) × adherence × min(1, horizon ÷ the paper's own duration), summed, clamped by MAX_CHANGE.ldl_cholesterol = 50, with the band from GRADE_SPREAD. Adherence is the figure on each Plan row: psyllium 86 %, oats 64 %, olive oil 85 %, the walk 57 %, resistance 33 %, plant sterols 71 % if it were adopted. The two dated draws on file are 168 mg/dL on Dec 9 2025 and 131 on Aug 1 2026; 118 is the Sep 1 2026 panel.

The hours. The dial places what is already on file: sleep 23:10 → 06:40 with one break at 03:20, meals at 08:15, 13:05 and 19:30, the selenium at 08:20, the steps total landing at 11:32, the post-meal walk due at 13:40, the resistance session 18:00 → 18:45. Nothing on the face is invented, and the size of a dish is its own calories.

The dishes. Seven photoreal plates generated with design-beyond/scripts/gen-image.sh on the face's own background, looked at one by one, cropped square, scaled to 300 px and inlined into this file as base64 in the window.IMG map. The page is one file: no network asset except the Google Fonts stylesheet.
