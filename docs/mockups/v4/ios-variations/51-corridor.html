Corridor

You against you. Every marker is a corridor built from your own earlier draws, with the lab's range as thin lines behind it and the last draw as a dot. Markers that leave their corridor rise to the top. Drag the scrubber back through the draws and watch each hunch light up on the day the rules would have raised it.

MoodA lab notebook kept by one careful person: plain rows, one shape repeated, the same plum header and grained cream as Hybrid. AccentPlum tint for your corridor, thin grey lines for the lab. Rose only for a dot outside your corridor, amber for a step or a drift, violet for a cluster, green for good news. Lime only on the scrub thumb and Play. TypeSpace Grotesk, five sizes: 11, 13, 17, 21, 34, plus the 55 count. Unusual ideaThe corridor is computed live. Scrub to any draw and the app forgets everything after it: corridors rebuild from the draws before, and signals fire only if the rules would have fired then.

The rules this file runs

Piece Rule Source
Corridor Median of the draws before the one shown, sd = 1.4826 × MAD, floored at 5 % of the median. Needs 5 earlier draws, else "not enough draws yet". The dark stripe is median ± 1 sd; the soft corridor is median ± 2.5 sd, the edge where a hunch starts. Spec A, bandOf
Outside |z| ≥ 2.5 against that corridor. These rows rise to the top. Spec B1.1
Step The last k ≥ 2 draws all above the earlier maximum or all below the earlier minimum, with at least 3 earlier draws. Spec A, stepOf
Drift OLS slope over the last three draws inside 24 months of the scrubbed day, past a per-marker minimum. The minimums here are placeholders. Spec A, recentSlope; table ◐ C
Cluster Ferritin, B12, vitamin D and homocysteine: at least 3 of 4 moving the store-low way over their recent draws, and at least one of them raising its own step or drift. Spec B1.5, my reading ○
Good news An earlier draw outside the corridor, then the last two draws inside it and on the other side of the median. Spec B1.7, my reading ○
Row order Outside, then step, then drift, then cluster, then inside, then too few draws, then not drawn yet. Moves with FLIP on every scrub.
