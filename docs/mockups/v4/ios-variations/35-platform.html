Variation 35 · Platform

Fifty-two seats on one board.
Four of them are booked.

A train-booking kit lent this screen its manners: white cards floating on a cool grey ground, one green for anything you can act on, and a seat map that already knew how to show four states at once. A goal is a leg of a journey, so it is drawn as one: where you are now, how far is left, where you are going.
