Deck

The home is a hand of five cards. One idea per card, the next ones peeking behind. Swipe to deal the next. On the move card the swipe is the answer: right ticks, left skips. Come back round to card one and the score has moved.

The deck's rules

Rule What it does
One idea per card Score, food, next move, blood, genes. Nothing shares a card.
Swipe deals A swipe past 89 px throws the top card; it goes to the back and the stack moves up. Under 89 px it springs back.
The move card answers Right is TICK (green stamp), left is SKIP (rose stamp). The stamp's opacity follows the drag. A tap on the target throws it right.
Score counts on return Card one keeps the number it last showed. When it comes back to the top it counts up to the new score, the stacked bar grows, and a pill says why.
Five tabs over the deck The glance: score, kcal, next move, LDL landing, APOE. Tap one to cut the deck to that card. The dots under the deck show the position.
Motion One spring (0.3, 1.35, 0.5, 1) at 480 ms for the stack; the throw at 420 ms; the score count at 900 ms.
