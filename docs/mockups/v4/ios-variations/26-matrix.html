Read from RonDesignLab's SuperPower Mobile: one score on a gradient that carries its own verdict, the number drawn as a dot matrix, then bento tiles where each domain leads with a real marker and its unit instead of a label. Two action tiles take the lab PDF and the tracker.

The gradient is the score: hue 30 (amber) at 0, hue 140 (green) at 100. Same arithmetic as Onion, same numbers. Lifestyle 77 · Blood 71 · Genes 63 → 72.

Gradient by score hue = 30 + (score/100) × 110, tile is linear-gradient(180deg, hsl(hue−25 80% 60%), hsl(hue+10 60% 55%)) 72 → hue 109. Amber at the bottom of the range, green at the top, so the colour is the verdict before the digits are read.
Matrix digits 5 × 7 bitmaps, dot diameter 0.7 of the cell, unlit dots at 12 % opacity, columns fading in on a 12 ms stagger The score and each tile's hero number. The unlit grid shows the measure has a ceiling.
Marker-first tiles Bento of four, glass over the domain tint at 10 % (life, blood, gene) Sleep 7:30, LDL 131 mg/dl, vitamin D 29 ng/ml, APOE ε3/ε4. The marker leads, the domain is the small print.
Action tiles Paper stack drawn in CSS, three rings pulsing on a 2.4 s loop Upload lab PDF and Connect Apple Health, the two ways blood and lifestyle get in. The upload tile opens the sheet.
