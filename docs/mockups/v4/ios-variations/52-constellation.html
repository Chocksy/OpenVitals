Every marker is a star. A star shines by how far its last draw sits from your own band, never from the lab's. Markers the data ties together are joined. Tap the constellation that lit up.

The read 16 characters: the owner's 16 draws. 12 letters for 12 systems: 7 lowercase are the 7 measured, 5 capitals the 5 still dark. 4 digits for 4 open hunches, and the last digit, 4, tops the brightness scale at z 4. Lm is lumens and Tz is a z: light equals z. k7 is kelvin: colour temperature gives direction. Vv is one letter in two cases: the same sky, seen twice. Mood A clear night where only your own changes shine. Accent Violet starlight #c4b0ff for the lit shape. Warm #ffb27a rises above your band, cool #8fd0ff falls below it. Type Space Grotesk, 11 13 16 21 34, tabular digits. Motion One easing, cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.24,1). 240, 470, 940 ms. Twinkle period 9 s, at 14 %. Cards None in the sky: hairlines only. Every star, from DATA

Band = median and 1.4826 × MAD of the draws before the last, floored at 5 % of the median (spec part A). Where DATA gives a band it is used, and the computed one is asserted against it. Ferritin and homocysteine have 4 prior draws and ApoB 2: the spec would wait for 5, so their stars are drawn with a thin ring meaning "thin band".
