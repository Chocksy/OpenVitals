Forecast

The body read as a synoptic chart, in the register of a weather service bulletin. Three kinds of fact, kept apart by the drawing: conditions now, the outlook, and climate. Two registers, serif for the voice and mono for every reading. Chart paper, one ink, one red on the single value that is off.

The field steps across the front, so the contours pack against it and open out in the plateaus. Eleven of them, every unit from 66 to 76, each labelled in a gap cut from its own line. Barbs carry the weights at one flag per ten: lifestyle four, blood four and a half, genes one and a half. The grammar is taught once, in the table below; the phone never re-explains itself.

The chart's grammar

Device How it is drawn What it carries
The field A gentle tilt, plus a 1.9-unit step across the front on a 40 px scale, plus the three domains as broad anomalies (v − 72) × 0.42 on a σ 95 px kernel. Marching squares on a 3 px grid, segments chained into whole contours, one Chaikin pass. The gradient is four times steeper at the front than in the western plateau, so the lines crowd where the change is and open where nothing is happening. Contours run off every edge.
Isobars Every unit from 66 to 76, eleven lines, every fourth heavier, each labelled horizontally in a gap cut just wide enough for it The gradient reads before the numbers do. The label never rotates, so it never reads upside down.
Station plot One circle diameter, four values in fixed slots, one barb length and one barb angle at every station. The blackened fraction of the circle is the domain index. Lifestyle 7:30 / 1497 / 93 / +2, blood 131 / 29 / 98 / +6, genes ε3/ε4 / C677T / 4 / 0.
Barb One flag per 10 and a half flag per 5, on weight × 100. Same shaft, same bearing, so only the feather count differs. Lifestyle four flags, blood four and a half, genes one and a half. The count is what says which domain moves the index most.
Cold front One spline, one triangle size at an even 44 px of arc, labelled off the chart edge on a leader LDL 168 → 131 since 9 Dec 2025, still passing.
Outlook No axis and no frame: a hairline median run, a soft wash for the spread, ticks on one even step along a single baseline, the target drawn as a dashed isobar with the same break-in-the-line label Keep this eight weeks and LDL lands near 104, from 131. The cone is the honest part.
Climate No box. A rule, a stamp, one line of ink. APOE ε3/ε4. It sets the range the forecast runs inside and is never in the run.
