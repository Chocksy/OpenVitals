Widgets

The home as a screen of iOS widgets plus a Live Activity. The Dynamic Island reads the plate while the widgets answer the four questions: the score in Activity rings, today's kcal against 1 900, the next move with a tick you press in place, and where LDL is going. Tap the tick: the lifestyle ring springs forward and 72 counts to 74.

MoodNative Apple, nothing invented: system grey, white widgets, one black Fitness widget.
AccentThe three Fitness ring colours carry the three layers; lime only on the plus and the island's reading bar.
TypeSF Pro Rounded (ui-rounded), Nunito where the browser has no rounded system face. Heavy numbers, tabular.

The direction's rules

Rule How
Widget sizes only Every block is a small (2 × 2), medium (4 × 2) or large (4 × 4) widget on a 13 px gutter. Nothing else sits on the grey.
One glance Above the tab bar: score (large), kcal (medium), next move and LDL (two smalls). Meals and the forecast are one scroll down.
Rings are Fitness rings 17 px stroke, 2 px gap, round caps, the start arrows, colour on a 22 percent track, black widget. Red lifestyle, green blood, blue genes.
Interactive widget The tick is an in-widget button, as iOS 17 allows. The widget then swaps to the next move with the system's blur-fade.
One spring Damping 0.6, 1 000 ms, written as CSS linear() from the spring equation (cubic-bezier overshoot where linear() is missing). Rings, the island, the sheet, the numbers.
Live Activity Expanded while reading (scan line over the photo, detected items arriving, lime bar), a green check when done, then compact: the rings and the score.
Add sheet Three equal Control Center tiles, a field, recent meals as chips. Send is hidden until there is a photo, a transcript or text.
