Shelves

One scrolling screen, three shelves sorted by what you can do with them. On top, what to do now: a fanned stack you swipe to tick. Then today so far, then where it's heading. The score never scrolls away: it sits in a plum header with its three layers and the last 21 days.

MoodA card table in daylight: cream paper, white cards, the plum score header as the one dark object. AccentPlum header, white cards on grained cream. Tangerine, berry and violet mark the three layers; day squares use the Matrix hue (30 + s/100 × 110). Lime only on the plus and Send. TypeSpace Grotesk, five sizes: 11, 13, 17, 21, 34, plus the 55 score. Unusual ideaA tick is physical: the swiped card flies up into the header, the lifestyle bar takes it and the score counts. Shelves lean with the scroll and settle on a spring.

The shelves' rules

Rule What it does
Three shelves, by verb Do now holds what you can act on. Today so far holds what is. Where it's heading holds what is predicted. A card never sits on two shelves.
Score stays put The plum header does not scroll. It shrinks the number from 55 to 34 once you scroll, and keeps the layer bars and the 21 days.
21 days in one row Streak's seeded days (mulberry32, seed 435), last 20 plus today, coloured hsl(30 + s/100 × 110, 68%, 90 − (s − 55) × 2.4 %). Tap a day for its score and reason. Today recolours on each tick.
Swipe to answer Right past 89 px: Done. The card flies into the score, the life bar grows, the score counts over 900 ms. Left: Later, the card goes to the back of the fan. The five dots in the shelf title untick with a tap.
Markers from blood Heading cards are every non-green row in BLOOD, sorted by how far the plan moves them, then PhenoAge. LDL lands at 104 from the levers (walks 9, oats 8, psyllium 10); tap a card to open it and toggle them.
A meal is editable Tap food: portion in quarters with the kcal recounted, macro rails against the day's target (the dark needle), rows swipe left to remove, Fix results offers corrections, Delete asks first.
Motion One spring (0.3, 1.35, 0.5, 1): 560 ms for cards, 700 ms for the shelf settle, 900 ms for bars and counts. Horizontal shelves lean with scroll speed, vertical shelves lag and settle in turn.
