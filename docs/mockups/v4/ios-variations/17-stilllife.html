Still life

Variation 17, round five, built through the design-beyond loop. The day is a still life: twelve dishes photographed from above and cut out of their background, set on a table with the numbers beside them the way a museum sets a wall label — the work, its date, one line of provenance. There is not one card on any screen. The plate's width is the square root of its calories, so the table itself is the chart. The day is a single hairline under the pictures, carrying a dot for each meal at the hour it was eaten. Play runs all four ways in: typing, the mic, one photograph (the plate loses its background as it is read) and four at once.

Direction

The read
The letter that repeats most in the seed falls at 4, 12 and 21 — gaps of eight and nine, never the same twice. That is the layout: the plates stagger by 0, 55, 13, 89 and 21 px and no two indents repeat. Its only two digits are 9 and 4, which differ by 5 and add to 13, and those are the measure the vertical rhythm is built on.
Mood
A table after a meal, photographed from above and labelled.
Scale
The plate is the number: its width is the square root of the calories, so the area on the table is the size of the meal. 412, 462 and 623 kcal come out at 134, 142 and 165 px. The label is always beside the plate, never under it, and always the same three lines: the dish in small caps, the calories where a wall label puts the date, and one line of provenance.
Accent
Cream, ink, and one burnt sienna: #8a3324 on the cream, #e19077 on the charcoal. It is allowed on a calorie number, the mark for now, and the projection with its landing dot. Nothing else on the page carries a hue — a state word is set in the serif italic rather than coloured. Lime is on the add control and on Send once there is something to send, and it is the only thing on the page that is neither ink nor sienna.
Imagery
Twelve dishes generated at 1024 and cut twice: once against the table's own cream with the real contact shadow kept, and once as the plate alone, keyed on the chromaticity that separates white china from cream in shade. The lit table uses the first, the dark table the second with the shadow drawn back in CSS, so the light falls the same way on either.
Type
Geist for what works, Newsreader for what is read, on three rungs and no more: 21 on a tooltip and the day's total, 34 on every value — a dish's calories and a marker's alike — and 44 on the day's name and the landing number.
Motion
One easing, cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). One entrance per plate, 760 ms, growing 14 % into place as if set down. Nothing else on the page moves.
The rule broken on purpose
The system's five type sizes. Newsreader runs at 21, 34 and 44, outside the ladder, because a wall label is set rather than sized, and the serif at 15 or 34 sans is neither.

Today, as a still life

Three plates, three labels, and the day underneath as one line. The pictures carry the calories; the label carries the hour and where the number came from. Tap a move and its check draws in, the count and the tick on the day line answer, and the sixth day closes.

Adding, all four ways

A system sheet, a plain field, a plain segmented control. Send is not in the document until there is something to send. Typing and the mic grow a dish into the still life above. The camera keeps the real photograph while it is being read, then drops the background: what is left is the cut-out that joins the table.
