Numbers from DATA Every reading, band, slope and heading word is the round-fifteen data pack, copied verbatim. Bands missing from DATA are computed here with the spec's bandOf (median, 1.4826 × MAD, floor 5 % of the median, needs 5 prior draws); homocysteine has 4, so it shows "no band yet". Your band vs the lab's Every marker chart shows the draws, the own band as a soft violet fill and the lab range as thin lines. A lab bound far off the scale is written at the edge instead of drawn.
Motion Hybrid's curves. The header opens on the 520 ms ease and the levels arrive on the 560 ms spring, 89 ms apart. The twelve arrows spring from flat to their angle, 55 ms apart. A system slides in from the right on the 560 ms spring; its bands fill, its lines draw in over 1.2 s and its dots pop. Explanation bars grow on the 900 ms spring.
