Casebook's case files on Corridor's charts, read at a glance first. Hunches live on Today as a slim shelf of rows: one plain line, one number, one mini corridor and one button that acts in place. Tap a row and the case slides up as a sheet with the corridor chart, the explanations and the test. Three depths

Depth Where What it holds
1 · Glance Today, Worth a look One row per hunch: a plain line, the number that matters with its marker and unit, a mini corridor (your corridor, its middle, the draw before and the last draw), and one button. The button is the first answer chip in short form, Book the test, or Got it. It acts in place: the row turns Noted, Written down or Seen and a toast confirms. No z-scores, no chances, no legend.
2 · Case a sheet over Today The stamp, the corridor chart (the draws, the band as it stood before each draw, the lab range behind it, the step or the slope marked), the explanations as bars with ● ◐ ○, every question chip (the bars re-order when one is picked), the test with its predictions, the written down 26 Sep stamp and Play result.
3 · How we know inside the sheet Five folds, closed by default, that open in place: the rule that fired with its numbers, the draws with the source file (and the report date where it differs), the evidence per explanation with grade and basis, what is not known, and a replay scrubber that walks the draws and lights the day the rule first fired.
URL ?depth=1|2|3 Opens all three phones at that depth. With no parameter: phone 1 at depth 1, phone 2 at depth 2, phone 3 at depth 3.
