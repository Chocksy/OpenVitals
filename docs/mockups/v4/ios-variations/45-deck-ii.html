Deck II

Deck kept its swipe and lost its five boxes. The score is pinned on top as a cream header with the last five weeks as squares; today's square changes with every tick. Under it three piles split by what you can do with a card: Do, Now, Ahead. The food card opens into an editor, and every change there reaches the ring and the score.

MoodThe same plum table after dark, now awake: paper grain on the cream cards, a light drifts across, the top card breathes. AccentCream cards on plum; tangerine, berry and violet for the three layers; the day squares in Matrix's red-to-green hue. Lime only for the plus, Send, Fix and the Do count. TypeSpace Grotesk: 11, 13, 17, 21, 28, 34 and the 55 score. GesturesSideways is always the card's own answer: on Do right ticks and left skips; on Now and Ahead either side sends the card to the back of its pile. Piles change with the label or a tap (or drag) on the neighbour stack peeking at the edge, so a tick can never turn into a page turn. Unusual ideaThe header is the calendar. Tap it and the five-week strip grows into 13 weeks with a tooltip per day; empty the Do pile and confetti falls while today's square flashes.

The rules

Rule What it does
Score header Always on top. The number rolls digit by digit, the state word and the week delta follow, the thin bar shows each layer's share. The five-week strip is Streak's seeded days in Matrix's hue; today's square pulses and takes the live score's colour.
Three piles Do: what you can act on (three open moves, a log prompt with a link to fix dinner). Now: food, sleep, markers as they stand (whatever BLOOD has off or borderline). Ahead: LDL landing, the PhenoAge trend, the Nov 1 draw.
Sideways answers Past 89 px the card flies. Do: right is TICK, left is SKIP (the log card: DONE, LATER). Now and Ahead: either way sends it to the back. Under 89 px it springs back.
Empty Do Cream confetti from the pile, today's square flashes, a teacup still bobs on the table with the chips to bring a skipped move back.
Food editor Tap a meal. Name inline, time, portion in 0.5× steps, macro tiles whose rails show the day against target, ingredient rows (tap the amount, swipe left to remove), Fix results ("it was two cans" doubles the tin), Delete with a confirm that crumples the sheet. The pill on the photo shows the day's kcal and the score live.
Dynamic Island The one place system feedback happens, true black like the hardware. At rest a compact pill: today's square in Matrix's hue and the score in cream. Send in the Add sheet grows it (Widgets II's spring, 620 ms): the photo thumb with a lime scan, the items it finds one by one, a lime ring and bar; then it shrinks and the meal drops into Now while the ring counts. Every swipe-right tick shows a receipt ("Walk after dinner · +2 · 74", 2.6 s); an empty Do pile shows "All done today" with the streak beside the confetti. One activity at a time: a tick during a read waits its turn.
Motion Stack spring (0.3, 1.35, 0.5, 1) at 480 ms, throw 420 ms, header roll 1.1 s, counts 700 ms.
