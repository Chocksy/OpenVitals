The stationery

Rule How it is held
Pitch is leading a 20 px pitch ruled 1 px at 15 px into it, every line box 20 px, so every written baseline lands on a printed rule
Printed where ruled the ruling and the six column rules are printed on the ledger block only. The head and the foot of the leaf are clear paper, so no printed line can cross a written word, and no head can read as an underlined link
Columns date 36, particulars 172, folio 30, credit 44, debit 44, in 8 px gutters with the rules down their centres. Particulars are left aligned, one strategy, with the printed reference range set inline
Rule weights hairline blue under every entry, a black double rule under a footing, a 2.5 px black rule at an account close and under the balance. The rules alone give the chapters
The margin the double margin rule at 47 and 49 px is the only rule that runs the full block height; the ruling starts to the right of it and never crosses it
The balance written last, at the foot, on clear paper, 64 px with its baseline on the block's third rule, closed by a double rule and a heavy one
Blood as double entry each marker credited 1.00, its shortfall debited 0.50 borderline or 1.00 off. The four in range are footed on one line. 7.00 against 2.00, net 5.00 of 7, which is the 71 the account posts
Designed to the height the leaf is 36 pitches from the status bar to the board and fills them: no blank ruled rows at the foot. The slip is 253 px so its perforation lands on a rule of the book beneath, never mid-entry
