Data, design and prototype notes

What changed against the current Today
The rail of three keeps its job as a set of doorways, but it stops being the densest thing on the screen. The four markers that carry a target move up into a two-up grid, each one showing value, unit, target band, the five draws behind it and the toGo line. That is the same data Api.Today.Goal already carries; the GoalCard ruler is one marker per card, this is four markers per screen.

The status card earns its size
It used to print three counts as text. It now prints them as a bar to scale, plus the same three counts across the last five draws, so a person with thousands of readings can see whether the picture is moving without leaving Today. Denominators are always explicit: 26 / 19 / 7 of 52.

Real
The counters 26 optimal / 19 normal / 7 off, the 52 markers and the Aug 1 2026 draw; the twelve systems with sex hormones never measured; TPO 320 → under 100 IU/mL, vitamin D 19 → 40–60 ng/mL, ferritin 22 → above 50 ng/mL, HbA1c 5.6 %; the protocol items and sardines at 0 % since Jun 14 2026; 7 234 steps, 27 HealthKit types, 08:12 sync; the five-slot tab bar and the lime capture control.

Illustrative
The five-draw history and the sparkline shapes are invented to show the form, not logged values. ApoB 112 and TSH 5.9 fill out the seven-off list. “On pace” and “not on pace” are printed only where onPace could exist; ferritin says “no projection yet” because a projection nobody made is not a no.

Prototype boundaries
One HTML file, no build, no network beyond the Google Fonts link the design system already uses. The reading-window control and the theme button are the only live behaviour; nothing fetches, and no screen here has an endpoint that does not already appear in ios.html’s data contract. Controls are 44 px, focus is visible, reduced motion removes transitions.

What this deliberately does not do
No streak, no badge, no score out of 100, no mood face row, no “Good morning” greeting illustration. A person with 52 markers off does not need to be congratulated; they need the four numbers with a target and the draws behind them.
