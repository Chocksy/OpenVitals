Corridors

The hunches live on the Blood tab. It opens on a glance you can read in two seconds: one plain line, one number, one small corridor and one button per hunch, then every marker as a slim corridor row with the ones outside your band on top. Tap a row and it opens in place with the chart, the explanations, the question and the test. Tap How we know and a page slides in with the rule, every draw and its file, the evidence, what is not known and a replay of the finding.

MoodCorridor's lab notebook with Casebook's rubber stamps, folded into three depths: read, open, check the sources. AccentPlum tint for your band (dashed when it is provisional), thin grey lines for the lab, green for the goal zone. Dot inks: berry above your band, amber for a drift, violet for the stores moving together, green for good news. Lime only on Play and the scrub thumb. TypeSpace Grotesk, 11, 13, 15, 17, 21, 55. Unusual ideaEvery number has a way down to its file. The glance row, the open chart and the draws table read the same DATA, and the replay forgets every draw after the scrubbed day.

Three depths, one rule each

Depth What it holds What it leaves out
1 · Glance The Blood tab. Hunches first: the title as the one plain line, the one number that matters, one mini corridor (your band, the draw before as a hollow dot, the last draw as a solid dot, the lab range as thin ticks) and one button: the question's first answer, "Book the test", or "Got it". Then every marker as a slim row, outside your band on top, the four stores joined by one violet rule. Today keeps one line, "4 things worth a look in your blood", that opens this tab (the badge on Blood). No z-scores, no percentages, no legend, no stamps.
2 · Open The row opens in place (FLIP, the rows below slide on the 620 ms spring). The stamp, the chart with the band as it stood before each draw, the step or the slope drawn in, the lab range behind; the explanations as bars with ● ◐ ○; the question with every answer (the bars re-order); the test, the predictions written down with the stamp, and Play. Sources and rule arithmetic.
3 · How we know A pushed page with sections closed by default: the rule that fired with its numbers, a replay of the finding (51's scrubber), every draw with its date, value and source file (the report date when it differs from the draw date, or "imported from the old app"), the evidence per explanation (grade, basis, one line), and what is not known. Laboratory names: none on file, none shown.
