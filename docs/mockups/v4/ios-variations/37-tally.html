Tally

The score as a bank statement drawn as a waterfall. It opens at 50, every row of the day posts a signed step, and the last bar is the balance: 72. Tick a move and a new credit posts, the balance bar grows, the number counts up. Tap any step for the row behind it.

MoodA statement you can audit: paper, one ink, every point accounted for.
AccentOne green for credits. Rose only on debits. Everything else is ink on paper white.
TypeJetBrains Mono for every number, tabular. Inter for words. Hairlines, no cards, no shadows.
Unusual ideaScore = 50 + Σ weight × (row − 50). The identity makes each row a step that sums exactly to the balance.

The statement: why the steps sum to the score

The weights add to 1, so score = 50 + Σ w × (layer − 50). Lifestyle is a mean of four rows, so each row carries 0.40 ÷ 4 = 0.10 per point. Moves are 20 points each: the planned five open the row at 0 (a −5.0 debit) and each tick posts +2.0.
