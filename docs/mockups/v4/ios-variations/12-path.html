How this concept works

Information architecture
Today: priorities, daily actions and updates. Path: goals, scenario, adopted plan and calendar. You: blood, body, genes, research and sources. Add is always within reach; a contextual question connects each explanation to the assistant.

Prediction contract
Scenario numbers are invented UI fixtures, not a medical model. Real estimates require validated effects, baseline trends, adherence, interactions and uncertainty. Genetics supplies context, never a deterministic response multiplier. A new blood draw verifies any predicted change.

Interaction & implementation
Tabs and details fade in over 220ms; sheets rise over 280ms; habit ticks settle over 180ms. Reduced motion removes all transitions. Controls are at least 44px. All three previews share session state. File, voice, Health and assistant flows are explicitly simulated; nothing leaves the browser.
