The shelves' rules

Rule What it does
Three shelves, by verb Do now holds what you can act on. Today so far holds what is. Where it's heading holds what is predicted. A card never sits on two shelves.
Score stays put The plum header does not scroll. Tap it and the 21 days give way to 13 weeks (Deck II's grid): tap a day for its score, layers and top reason. The blood draw carries a dot.
21 days in one row Streak's seeded days (mulberry32, seed 435), last 20 plus today, coloured hsl(30 + s/100 × 110, 68%, 90 − (s − 55) × 2.4 %). Tap a day for its score and reason. Today recolours on each tick.
Swipe to answer Right past 89 px: Done. The card flies into the score, the life bar grows, the score counts over 900 ms. Left: Later, the card goes to the back of the fan. The five dots in the shelf title untick with a tap.
Markers from blood Heading cards are every non-green row in BLOOD, sorted by how far the plan moves them, then PhenoAge. LDL lands at 104 from the levers (walks 9, oats 8, psyllium 10); tap a card to open it and toggle them.
A meal is editable Tap food: a sheet over the home with Deck II's editor. Portion in 0.5× steps, kcal and macros recount against the day, rows swipe left to remove, Fix results reads a note again ("it was two cans"), Delete asks first. Each change lands on the score above.
Focus The Do now button opens the table from the stack's own rectangle (clip-path and scale on the 560 ms spring). Right past 89 px ticks with a TICK stamp, left skips to the back. The chips tick any move. An empty pile throws 42 pieces of confetti.
Dynamic Island One activity at a time, from 47. Send in Add reads the plate with a ring and a bar. A tick shows its receipt for 2.6 s ("Walk after dinner · +2 · score 74"). The last tick shows All done and the streak. Otherwise it rests as rings and the score.
Motion One spring (0.3, 1.35, 0.5, 1): 560 ms for cards, 700 ms for the shelf settle, 900 ms for bars and counts. Horizontal shelves lean with scroll speed, vertical shelves lag and settle in turn.
