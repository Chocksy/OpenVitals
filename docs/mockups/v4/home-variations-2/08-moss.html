Where the numbers come from

Real, from the test account: 52 markers with a value (26 optimal · 19 normal · 7 off), TPO 320 IU/mL, vitamin D 19 ng/mL, HbA1c 5.6 %, ALT 34 U/L, LDL 142 mg/dL, twelve systems with sex hormones never measured, draws on Dec 9 2025 (48 results), Feb 1 2026 (1), Aug 1 2026 (15) and Aug 31 2026 (1), 17 protocol items with 0 ticked today and a 5-day streak. Illustrative, with real shapes and units: the Apple Health day (8 412 steps, 34 min exercise, 7 h 12 sleep) and the resting-HR series, 90 daily values whose two printed numbers are the real ones the engine reports, 58 bpm thirty days ago and 55 today, against a 90-day mean of 58. The phone synced on 86 of the 90 days; the four gaps are Jun 17, Jul 4, Jul 26 and Aug 19.

Moss is good at making the status countable. The ring is 52 arcs, one per marker, so 7 coral out of 52 is a quantity you can read at a glance instead of a mood, and the coral never has to work alone: the word “Attention”, the word “off” on every pill and the count line all say it in text. Dark green reads as a resting screen rather than a dashboard, and sky blue is reserved for one thing only, the data itself. In the app it costs a small generator for the ring (52 arc paths, three colour bands) and one gradient behind it, plus a real font budget: Fraunces is a variable face with three axes and Instrument Sans adds a second family. The systems row is cheap, a flex-wrap of pills with no fixed grid to maintain. On iOS the ring is a natural Swift Charts or Canvas drawing and the pills map to a wrapping layout; the dark palette needs a light counterpart before ship, the 88 px numeral has to shrink under Dynamic Type, and the coral on deep green must be checked against Increase Contrast.
