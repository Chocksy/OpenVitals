Build cost

Replaces: /labs (LabsHeader, UploadButton, a <details> per draw day, the marker table), /biomarkers (search, per-category sections, status dot, sparkline, RangeBar), /labs/phone (the phone metric list), /uploads and /uploads/[id] (GenomeTable, DocumentItems, ReadingRows, the raw <pre>), /m/[code] (the ruler card, the recharts TrendChart, the readings table, the inline optimal and goal forms) and /genome.

New: the marker row carries its own range scale, so a bar is readable without opening the marker. A marker with one draw says “one draw” instead of drawing a flat sparkline. The drawer and the phone page are the same component at two widths.

Deletes: recharts from /m/[code], two dashed empty states, StatusBadge and the filled health badges, the second tab style, and seven routes.
