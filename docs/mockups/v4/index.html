Phase 31b — what makes it useful

Drawn Sep 3 2026 for review. Five new pages and one revised section, all on system.css. Each one ends with where its numbers come from and a build-cost note naming the tables and endpoints that exist, the ones that do not, and what the engine must gain.

Research
New for you: papers matched to your conditions, each with what it found and what it would move — or “nothing for you”. Research now, per condition. The empty state that says nothing is new.
Plan · the month
A month you can see, a Today column in the order the day runs, the every-day rules, the supplements as a schedule with a dose and a stop date, and what is coming.
Genome, rewritten
Answer first. Coeliac essentially excluded, no test needed. Haemochromatosis excluded. Type 2 diabetes +40 % from one allele. The rsids one disclosure down.
Upload detail, revised
A read receipt: 16 variants read, 11 with a known effect, 3 that moved a likelihood, 2 that closed a question — and one line saying there is nothing for you to do.
iOS
The phone as a native app: Today, Body, Meals, Capture, Plan, Settings — and a data contract table saying which endpoint each screen needs and which two of six exist.
iOS · second pass
The phone after a week of use: a week strip and a streak, goals as rows, moves as tickable chips, the whole Add flow in seven frames with the loader and the receipt, meal detail with edit and delete, and the plan split into Doing and Suggested.
Topic
Creatine, one page: the verdict strip per outcome, what the trials found with dose and n, what is only an association, your own grip number, and the found-not-read-yet state.
Chart hover
The card on every chart mark and every ruler: date, value, unit, state word, the band behind the word, and the was. Hover on desktop, tap on the phone.
