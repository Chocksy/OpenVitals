03

The honesty rule

No decorative data anywhere in this folder. Every dot, tick, diamond and band is a thing you can name and date. The test user's blood values, draw dates, systems, conditions, likelihoods, protocol items and streaks are real as of Sep 1 2026. Apple Health day values and the daily resting-HR series are illustrative, with real shapes and units, and every page that uses them says so at the bottom.
