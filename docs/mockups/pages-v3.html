Phase 28b-3. Same tokens, same rulers, same glyphs as home-v3 and elements-v3. This sheet is the level above: whole screens (Blood, Uploads, Graph, Plan, Genome, Papers) and the multi-step flows (ask → discuss → the engine asks back, suggest → pick → order sheet → goal).

Same rule: no decorative data. Every diamond, dot, circle and number is a thing you can name. The test user's blood values, draw dates, systems, conditions and likelihoods are real (Sep 1 2026). Everything illustrative is listed in the footnote at the end.

05

Ask, and discuss

The pill grows a thread underneath it. Every answer names the state it is answering from, cites what it read, and ends in something you can press. Five frames, in order.

1 Idle

Ask anything, or tell me something about you

One field, two jobs. A question gets an answer; a statement gets a receipt.

2 Asked

Should I take selenium?
Right now: Hashimoto's — confirmed, 95 %

Probably yes, for twelve weeks, then retest. Selenium 200 mcg/day lowered TPO antibodies by about 12 % in people who looked like you, and your TPO is the loudest number you have at 320 IU/mL against a ceiling of 34.

It does not treat the thyroid itself. Your TSH is 3.9 mIU/L, which is inside the lab range and outside the optimal one, so the thing to watch after twelve weeks is both numbers, not the antibodies alone.

Sources ● B · Toulis 2010 · meta-analysis, k = 9 ● B · Wichman 2016 · pooled ◐ · Clinician podcast 2024
Act on it

The state line is not decoration: it is the card the answer was written from. If it is wrong, the answer is wrong, and you can see that in one glance.

TCF7L2 rs7903146 C/T ● A 36 % of Europeans carry at least one T

You carry one copy of the T allele. This is the strongest common variant for type 2 diabetes anyone has found: it changes how much insulin your pancreas releases after a meal, not how your muscles answer it. One copy raises the starting odds by about 40 %.

What it changes for you: your T2D likelihood started at 38 % rather than 27 % before any blood was read. Your HbA1c of 5.6 and your missing fasting insulin are doing the rest of the work.

Type 2 diabetes ×1.4 per T allele ● A · Grant 2006 · Nat Genet · replicated
A variant shifts a starting point; your numbers decide the rest. If a marker and a variant disagree, the marker wins, because it is what your body is doing today.

Opinion, anecdote, and you

The weak tiers stay visible, because hiding them means you go and find them somewhere worse. Each one carries who said it and the single study that would upgrade it. Your own result outranks both.

◆ you · n = 1

Selenium 200 mcg, 12 weeks. TPO 320 → 280, −12 %.

Adherence 86 % from your habit ticks. This is one cycle; two make it count toward your own projections, and it already outranks every opinion below.

◐ opinion · clinician podcast, 2024

"Everyone with Hashimoto's should be on selenium, 200 mcg, for life"

Dose stated: 200 mcg/day, indefinitely. The pooled trials ran 3 to 12 months and none tested "for life", so the dose is borrowed from them and the duration is not.

Would upgrade this A trial that keeps taking TPO past 12 months, or one that stops the selenium and watches it come back. Either moves this to ● B.
○ anecdotal · forum thread, 41 replies

"Going gluten free dropped my antibodies from 600 to 90"

No dose, no dates, no control, and the people whose antibodies did not move do not post. The one RCT nearby is ● C, n = 34, and its band crosses zero.

Would upgrade this A replication of Krysiak 2019 at n > 200, or your own 12-week trial with a TPO at each end, which is a thing you can actually run.

The order is the point: your outcome, then opinion, then anecdote. Nothing is deleted and nothing is promoted.

The rule. The hue follows the worst state band on the ledger: all optimal or normal is green, one or more off is amber, a confirmed condition with an overdue action or a value past a critical line is rose. The saturation follows freshness: every draw older than its own cadence drains it, and nothing at all turns the gradient off.
